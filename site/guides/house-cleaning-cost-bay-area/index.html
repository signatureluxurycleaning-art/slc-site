What actually determines the price

Your home, exactly. We price by the exact count of bedrooms and bathrooms - never ranges like "2-3 bedrooms", which always end with someone overpaying. Houses also factor in square footage, because a 3-bedroom in 2,800 sq ft is not the same job as a 3-bedroom in 1,400 sq ft.

The first visit. The first regular or deep cleaning of a new home carries a one-time setup rate - the first visit is always the heaviest. From the second cleaning on, you pay the table price as long as you keep a regular schedule.

Frequency. Weekly service is 15% off and bi-weekly is 10% off the per-visit price. One-time cleanings pay full table price.

Timing. Same-day / urgent evening cleanings carry a 15% surcharge. Booking a day or more ahead always costs less.

Why Bay Area cleaning costs more than the national average

National cost guides quote figures that simply do not survive contact with Silicon Valley: local wages, insurance and the size of homes here all run well above the national average. Directories and lead marketplaces respond by showing teaser rates, then quoting higher once someone is in your home.
