Frequently Asked Questions

What is the White Glove Deep Clean?

White Glove Deep Clean is Signature Luxury Cleaning's premium service. A 2-cleaner team works for a minimum of 6 hours and physically moves your refrigerator, stove, washer, dryer, sofas, and beds to clean every hidden surface. Includes before/after photo documentation. Starts at $750 for a 2-bedroom home.

What's the difference between Deep Cleaning and White Glove Deep Clean?

Standard Deep Cleaning covers inside appliances, baseboards, and detailed surfaces. White Glove Deep Clean adds physically moving heavy appliances (refrigerator, stove, washer, dryer) and furniture (sofas, beds, dressers) to clean behind and underneath. White Glove requires a 6-hour minimum and 2-cleaner team.

How far in advance do I need to book?

White Glove Deep Clean requires at least 24-hour advance booking. Same-day service is not available for this premium tier due to the 6-hour minimum duration and 2-cleaner team scheduling. We recommend booking 3-7 days in advance for preferred dates.

How much does it cost?

Pricing starts at $750 for a 2-bedroom/2-bathroom home and goes up to $1,449 for a 5-bedroom/4-bathroom home. Homes with 6+ bedrooms require a custom quote. Half-bath surcharge is $15. Additional refrigerator/freezer or washer/dryer pair: +$75 each.

Is this available for apartments or offices?

White Glove Deep Clean is exclusively a residential service for houses and townhomes. It is not available for apartments (due to appliance moving constraints) or commercial/office spaces.
