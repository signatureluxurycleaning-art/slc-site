Why have a cleaning company unpack your home?

An empty cabinet is the one moment it can be cleaned properly. That is the whole idea behind this service: we are a house cleaning company, so before your dishes, clothes and linens go in, the inside of every cabinet, drawer and closet is cleaned. You start life in your new home with everything in its place, and everything clean behind it.

🧽 Cleaned before it is filled

Shelves, drawers and closets are cleaned inside before a single item goes in. It is the one step that is hard to go back and do once the shelves are full.

👥 A team, not one helper

Three professionals or more work through your home together, so the kitchen, bedrooms and bathrooms move forward at the same time.

🏡 Owner-operated and insured

Signature Luxury Cleaning is a small, owner-operated house cleaning company. Our team is background-checked, bonded and insured, and you always talk to the owners.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in Move-In Unpacking & Organizing?

Our team unpacks your boxes and puts everything in its place, room by room: kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms and closets. Before anything goes in, we clean the inside of every cabinet, drawer and closet, so your home is clean and set up from day one. We also break down the empty boxes as we go.

How much does unpacking cost?

$75 per hour per professional, with a minimum team of 3 professionals and a minimum of 3 hours, so visits start at $675. Before you book, the owners text you an estimate of hours and cost for your home.

How long does it take to unpack a home?

It depends on the size of your home and the number of boxes. Send us your details and the owners will text you an estimate of hours for your home. Larger homes can take more than one day.

What is not included?

Furniture assembly and electronics setup are not included. We also ask that you unpack jewelry and important documents yourself.

Do I need to be home while you unpack?

It helps a lot. Being there at the start lets you show us where you would like things to go. With your boxes labeled by room, we take it from there.

Can you also clean the home before we move in?

Yes. Our Move-In Cleaning is a complete top-to-bottom clean of the empty home, best booked before the moving truck arrives. Unpacking & Organizing then sets up each room.

Which areas do you serve?

Silicon Valley and the Peninsula, from Burlingame to San Jose, including Los Altos, Los Altos Hills, Palo Alto, Menlo Park, Mountain View, Redwood City, San Carlos, Belmont and San Mateo.
