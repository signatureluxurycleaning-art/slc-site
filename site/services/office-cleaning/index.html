Frequently asked questions

What types of commercial spaces do you clean?

Small and mid-size offices, medical and dental practices, law and professional suites, salons and studios, real estate offices, co-working spaces, retail stores and showrooms — across Palo Alto, Menlo Park, Mountain View, Redwood City, San Carlos, Belmont and the Peninsula.

How does commercial cleaning pricing work?

Every space is different, so we start with a free on-site walkthrough: one of the owners visits, understands exactly what you need, and sends a custom quote — usually within 24 hours. Recurring contracts receive preferred rates.

Can you clean our office in the evening or on weekends?

Yes — that's our specialty. We clean in the evening after business hours, and on Saturdays and Sundays, so your team never works around a cleaning crew. Daytime cleaning is also available when it suits your business.

Do you offer recurring office cleaning contracts?

Yes. Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly contracts are available with preferred rates, priority scheduling and the same trusted crew on every visit — the team learns your space.

Are you insured and bonded for commercial work?

Yes. We carry $1M/$2M general liability insurance and a $10,000 janitorial services bond, and every team member is background-checked. A Certificate of Insurance is available on request for your building or property manager.
