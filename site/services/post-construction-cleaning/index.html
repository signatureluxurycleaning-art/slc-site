Frequently asked questions

What is included in post-construction cleaning?

Removal of all construction dust, drywall dust, debris, paint overspray, adhesive residue; windows cleaned inside and out; deep cleaning of all bathrooms and kitchen; grout haze removal; all floors vacuumed and mopped; final inspection for move-in readiness.

How much does post-construction cleaning cost in Silicon Valley?

Post-construction cleaning starts at $410 for smaller spaces and goes up to $900+ for large homes. Price depends on square footage and extent of construction.

How long does post-construction cleaning take?

Typically 6–12 hours depending on the size of the space and extent of construction. We bring a team of 2 or more cleaners to complete the job efficiently.

Do you clean after kitchen or bathroom remodels?

Yes. Kitchen and bathroom remodels are our most common post-construction jobs. We remove grout haze, tile adhesive, drywall dust, and all construction debris.
