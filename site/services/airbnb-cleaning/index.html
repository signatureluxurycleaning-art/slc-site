Frequently asked questions

What is included in Airbnb turnover cleaning?

All rooms cleaned and sanitized, kitchen cleaned, all bathrooms scrubbed, fresh linens and towels set up, beds made hotel-style, trash emptied, floors vacuumed and mopped, and a final inspection to ensure the property is guest-ready.

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Silicon Valley?

Airbnb cleaning starts at $155 for a studio or 1-bedroom and goes up to $270 for a 3-bedroom house property. Recurring host discounts available.

Can you clean between same-day guest checkouts and check-ins?

Yes. We specialize in fast turnovers between same-day checkouts and check-ins. With a 2-cleaner team, we can typically complete a 1–2 bedroom turnover in 2–3 hours. Book at least 24 hours in advance.

Do you provide linens and supplies?

We bring all cleaning supplies and equipment. Linens and towels are typically provided by the host. Discuss linen service options when you book.
