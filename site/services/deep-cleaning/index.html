What does deep cleaning include?

A thorough top-to-bottom restoration of your entire home — every surface, every corner, every room. Below is exactly what our team covers on every deep clean visit.

🍳 Kitchen

  • ✓ Inside and outside of oven — degreased and cleaned
  • ✓ Inside of refrigerator — cleaned and sanitized
  • ✓ Microwave inside and outside
  • ✓ Outside of all appliances — stainless steel polished
  • ✓ Inside and outside of cabinets and drawers
  • ✓ Countertops, sink and faucets sanitized
  • ✓ Backsplash and tiles degreased
  • ✓ Floor vacuumed and mopped
  • ✓ Trash can cleaned and sanitized

🛁 Bathrooms

  • ✓ Toilet — inside and outside, including base
  • ✓ Shower, bathtub and glass doors — hard water/lime scale removal
  • ✓ Sink and mirror polished
  • ✓ Grout scrubbed and cleaned
  • ✓ Inside of cabinets under the sink
  • ✓ Bathroom vent vacuumed
  • ✓ Floor washed and disinfected
  • ✓ Towel bars and toilet paper holder sanitized

🛏️ Bedrooms & Living Areas

  • ✓ Dust removed from all surfaces — frames and pictures included
  • ✓ Ceiling fans cleaned
  • ✓ Inside of closets and wardrobes
  • ✓ Baseboards and trim cleaned
  • ✓ Interior window sills
  • ✓ Light fixtures and lampshades vacuumed
  • ✓ Bed made and linens changed (upon request)
  • ✓ Hard floors vacuumed and mopped / carpets vacuumed

🏠 Whole House

  • ✓ Light switches cleaned and disinfected
  • ✓ Door handles and knobs sanitized
  • ✓ Interior windows + tracks cleaned
  • ✓ AC units / filters vacuumed from outside
  • ✓ Air vent covers vacuumed
  • ✓ Stairs (if applicable) — fully cleaned
  • ✓ Entryway tidied and organized
  • ✓ Home freshened with a light scent at the end
