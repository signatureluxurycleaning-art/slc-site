FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does house cleaning cost?

Regular cleaning starts at $170/visit (monthly). With weekly service, new clients pay as low as $145/visit. Your first visit includes a one-time setup rate (~25%). Deep cleaning starts at $270.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes! We bring all supplies and equipment. Choose between regular or eco-friendly products at no extra cost.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are fully licensed, insured, bonded, and all team members pass background checks.

How do I book a cleaning?

Book instantly through our app or call (650) 619-3504. Most clients get same-day or next-day availability.

What areas do you serve?

We serve 22 cities across the Bay Area: San Jose, Palo Alto, Mountain View, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Fremont, and more.

Do you offer recurring discounts?

Yes! New clients who choose recurring service save on every visit: weekly −15%, bi-weekly −10%.

Can I book a one-time cleaning?

Yes. Deep cleaning, post-construction, move in/out — all available without commitment.

How long does a cleaning take?

A 3-bedroom home takes 2-3 hours with our team of two. Deep cleaning may take 4-5 hours.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients use keyless entry or smart locks. Manage everything through our app.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 48 hours before your appointment. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 30% fee, per our Terms of Service.
